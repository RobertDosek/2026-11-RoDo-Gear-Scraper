Results Keywords

Scans read a category's listing and keep every ad. Keywords are matched against the ad titles from the latest scans, ignoring case and accents, so a new keyword shows its ads straight away. A keyword's words can appear in any order. With a price limit set, ads without a price (cena dohodou) are left out.

Direct (the box on the left of a keyword): its new matches also go to your phone, all of them or with price based only those up to your price. The prepared answer on the right is what the seller gets; without one you only get the alert.

Scan new ads reads only what was posted since the category's last scan. Full scan reads every page and also finds sold ads and price changes. Every hour each category gets a new-ads scan (plus a full scan once a day), and every ad you watch, wait on or haven't decided on yet is opened to check its price and whether it was sold.
